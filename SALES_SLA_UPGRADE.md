# Sales Response SLA Upgrade

The Sales Cockpit now surfaces the existing 15-minute lead-response SLA directly above the priority action queue.

- Fresh leads show time remaining.
- Uncontacted leads at 15+ minutes are marked as breached.
- Sales users get Call, WhatsApp, and Open Lead actions.
- The panel refreshes every 60 seconds.
- Existing inventory and CRM data flows remain unchanged.
